3b Research Tree

Every faction shares this same research tree once their Research Facility is built (item 4h below). Each item has 3 levels a faction can research in order, from Level 1 up - set what percentage bonus each level actually gives; leave one at 0 to make that level a no-op. Mining Rate's Level 1 also unlocks the Mining Ship itself, same as always; Rapid Repair Crews boosts a Field Hospital medic's heal amount (item 4e below); Extended Range boosts every unit's and Gun Turret's own attack/heal range.

3c Terrain Obstacles (optional)

Boulders, mountains, wreckage, whatever fits your theme - solid scenery units can't walk through. Add one row per obstacle TYPE, upload art (or pick one from the shared library) or leave it blank for a generic mountain marker, then click the map below each one to place as many copies of it as you like. Units automatically steer around whatever you place instead of getting stuck on it.

4f Missile Silo (optional)

A stationary long-range launcher. Works like Air Base above for the building itself - name it whatever fits (Missile Silo, Launch Bay…), give it its own art, HP, and build cost. The launcher you add below it is placed differently from Air Base's own units though: like a Gun Turret, building one arms a placement mode - click anywhere between 50 and 300 range of your Home Base to place it there. It never moves once placed, and never fires on its own: click it, then click an enemy, to send a missile at that target. The missile takes real time to travel there (set the missile's own speed per launcher, below) and locks onto whatever it was fired at - if that target is destroyed before the missile arrives, it just disappears from the screen instead of exploding on nothing. Its range starts at 500 and researches up to 1000 then 2000 in-game (see the Research tab) - not a field set here, same idea as Gold's own mining rate upgrading through research. Leave the launcher list empty and this whole feature just doesn't show up in-game.

4i Gun Turrets (optional)

Stationary defenses - not built next to your Home Base automatically like other units. Instead, in-game, building one puts you into a placement mode: click anywhere between 50 and 300 range of your Home Base to place it there. It never moves once placed (Speed is ignored for these), and fires on its own at whatever enemy comes into range. Add as many turret types as you want; leave the list empty and this feature just doesn't show up in-game.

4j Religious Institution (optional)

A building for Clerics - support units that convert an enemy unit to your side instead of fighting. Works like Air Base above for the building itself (name it whatever fits, give it its own art, HP, and build cost, produced with the same countdown/queue as every other building). Each Cleric you add below has no attack at all: HP, convert range, speed, and cost. Once within range, a Cleric converts one enemy unit each turn (Turn-Based) or each round (Real-Time) - the converted unit keeps its own stats but switches sides and fights for its new owner from then on. Mining Ships and other Clerics can never be converted. Leave the Cleric list empty and this whole feature just doesn't show up in-game.
