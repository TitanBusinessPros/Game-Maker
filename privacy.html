Privacy Policy

Titan Game Maker · Last updated September 16, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Titan Game Maker (index.html, play.html, admin.html, and any map or game you create or download using them — the "Service") collects, how it's used, and where it's stored. The Service is operated by Titan Business Pros LLC. It has no dedicated backend server of its own beyond a single small Cloud Function described below — it runs on Google's Firebase platform and is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages.

2. Information We Collect

WhatWhen
Your Google account email address and profile info provided by Google Sign-InSigning in to index.html or admin.html (required to save/download a map or, for the one admin account, manage the shared library)
Map data you enter (names, unit/building stats, faction setup, map size, etc.)Whenever you build or edit a map
Art and sound files you uploadWhenever you fill an upload slot in index.html or admin.html
Your download-credit balance and a record that a given Stripe payment session was already processed (no card numbers) Automatically, as you use download credits or purchase more
Your Firebase user ID (a Google-issued identifier, not your email) as the client_reference_id on a Stripe purchase linkIf you click "Get 10 credits for $5"

We do not operate any tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels of our own inside the Service. Google's own sign-in library and Firebase's SDKs may set their own session/local storage as part of making authentication work; that is between you and Google and governed by Google's privacy policy.

3. How We Use Information

  • To let you sign in, and to associate your maps and download-credit balance with your own account.
  • To store and serve back the art, sound, and map data you upload so your map can be edited, played, and downloaded.
  • To enforce the download-credit system (starting balance, one credit per download, granting 10 more after a verified Stripe payment).
  • To operate the shared asset library (for our one admin account only).
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where Data Is Stored

All Service data is stored on Google's Firebase platform (project game-maker-ed014), specifically:

  • Firestore — your map data, the shared library's item list, the admin's bonus-credit email list, and your download-credit balance.
  • Cloud Storage — the art/sound files you or the admin upload.
  • Cloud Functions — a single function that receives Stripe's payment-confirmation webhook and grants download credits; it verifies Stripe's signature before doing anything and does not otherwise process or store payment details itself.

Access to your own map data and account is restricted by Firestore and Storage security rules to your signed-in Google account (or, for library management, to our one admin account).

5. Public Nature of Map and Library Data

To keep the Service simple and serverless, saved maps (maps/{'{'}mapId{'}'}) and shared-library items are stored with public read access at the database/storage level — the same setting that lets your downloaded standalone game load its own art and sound with no sign-in required. This means the raw data and file URLs for a map or library asset can technically be read by anyone who has or discovers their address, even though the Service's own interface never publishes a public browsing page for them and search engines are not directed to index them. Only your account's write access (creating, editing, or deleting your own maps) and your download-credit balance are restricted to you specifically. Do not upload or enter anything into a map that must be kept confidential.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google / Firebase — authentication (Google Identity Services) and all data storage described above. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — processes payments for extra download credits. Your payment card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the Service's static files and may log standard web-server request data (e.g. IP address) per GitHub's Privacy Statement.

7. Downloaded Standalone Games

A game downloaded via "⬇ Download My Game" is a self-contained HTML file with all art and sound inlined into it. Once downloaded, it makes no calls back to Titan Business Pros or to our Firebase project at all, and collects no information about whoever plays it. It does still load the Firebase SDK itself from Google's own gstatic.com content-delivery network when opened, which is unrelated to this Service's own data and governed by Google's own policies, not this one.

8. Data Retention

We keep your account, map, and library data for as long as your account exists or the data serves the purposes above. You may request deletion of your account data at any time using the contact information below; we will delete what we control, other than records we're required to keep (e.g. for fraud prevention or legal compliance).

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your Choices

You can review or delete the maps saved under your account from the "My Maps" section of index.html. To request a copy or deletion of any other data we hold about you, contact us below.

11. Security

We rely on Firebase Authentication and Firestore/Storage security rules to restrict who can write to your account's data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after a change is posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
