Map

Left-drag a box to select multiple units, or click one.
Click empty space to move selected units there.
Click an enemy to attack it.
Right-drag to pan the camera. Scroll wheel to zoom in/out.
Click the minimap to jump the camera there - with units selected, it also sends them there.
A unit that attacks locks in place (can't take a new move order, still keeps firing) until the next turn.
A Mining Ship parked on your deposit earns resources automatically.
The match ends when a homeworld's HP reaches 0 - your whole team wins together.

Choose Teams

Every faction is on a team. You're always Team A - give each other faction any team letter: the same as yours to fight alongside you, or its own letter to fight independently (up to one team per faction, for a real free-for-all).

At least one other faction needs a different team than yours - otherwise there's no one to fight.
