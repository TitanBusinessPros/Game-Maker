Privacy Policy

Titan Game Maker · Last updated September 20, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Titan Game Maker (index.html, play.html, admin.html, producer.html, and any map or game you create or download using them — the "Service") collects, how it's used, and where it's stored. The Service is operated by Titan Business Pros LLC. It has no dedicated backend server of its own beyond a few small Cloud Functions described below — it runs on Google's Firebase platform and is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages.

index.html/admin.html/producer.html can also be installed as a home-screen app on a phone or desktop. This uses your browser's own service worker feature to cache the Service's static app files locally on your device (for a basic offline fallback screen only); that cache lives entirely in your browser, is never transmitted to us, and collects no additional information.

3. How We Use Information

  • To let you sign in, and to associate your maps and download-credit balance with your own account.
  • To store and serve back the art, sound, and map data you upload so your map can be edited, played, and downloaded.
  • To enforce the download-credit system (starting balance, one credit per download, granting 10 more after a verified Stripe payment).
  • To operate the shared asset library (for our one admin account only) and the Premium Library, including tracking which Premium items your account has unlocked.
  • To let you save and resume in-progress Titan Game Producer projects, and to combine your downloaded maps into one compiled game.
  • To send you our newsletter and other emails about the Service, new features, or Titan Business Pros — see Section 4 below.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Email Communications

By signing in with your Google account and using the Service, you agree that we may use the email address associated with that account to send you our newsletter and other communications about the Service, including new features, updates, and offers. You can opt out of these emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in each one, or by contacting us directly (Section 14). Opting out of marketing emails does not opt you out of essential account or transactional messages (e.g. those related to a purchase or your account itself).

5. Where Data Is Stored

All Service data is stored on Google's Firebase platform (project game-maker-ed014), specifically:

  • Firestore — your map data, the free and Premium Library item lists, the admin's bonus-credit email list, a queue of pending credit grants for emails that haven't signed in yet, your download-credit balance, your saved Titan Game Producer drafts, and which Premium Library items your account has unlocked.
  • Cloud Storage — the art/sound files you or the admin upload, your saved Titan Game Producer stage files/cover art, and Premium Library assets.
  • Cloud Functions — three functions. One receives Stripe's payment-confirmation webhook and grants download credits after a purchase; it verifies Stripe's signature before doing anything and does not otherwise process or store payment details itself. The second lets only our admin account manually add download credits to a specific account (or queue them for an email that hasn't signed in yet) — for example, as a promotion or in response to a support request — independent of any Stripe payment. The third spends one of your download credits to permanently unlock a Premium Library item for your account, recording the unlock in the same atomic step as the credit deduction.

Access to your own map data and account is restricted by Firestore and Storage security rules to your signed-in Google account (or, for library management, to our one admin account).

6. Public Nature of Map and Library Data

To keep the Service simple and serverless, saved maps (maps/{mapId}) and free and Premium Library items are stored with public read access at the database/storage level — the same setting that lets your downloaded standalone game load its own art and sound with no sign-in required, and that lets a locked Premium item still show its real thumbnail before you unlock it. This means the raw data and file URLs for a map or library asset (including a Premium item's) can technically be read by anyone who has or discovers their address, even though the Service's own interface never publishes a public browsing page for them and search engines are not directed to index them. Your Titan Game Producer drafts, which Premium items your account has unlocked, your account's write access (creating, editing, or deleting your own maps or drafts), and your download-credit balance are all restricted to you specifically, not public. Do not upload or enter anything into a map or draft that must be kept confidential.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Google / Firebase — authentication (Google Identity Services) and all data storage described above. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — processes payments for extra download credits. Your payment card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the Service's static files and may log standard web-server request data (e.g. IP address) per GitHub's Privacy Statement.

8. Downloaded Standalone Games

A game downloaded via "⬇ Download My Game" is a self-contained HTML file with all art and sound inlined into it. Once downloaded, it makes no calls back to Titan Business Pros or to our Firebase project at all, and collects no information about whoever plays it. It does still load the Firebase SDK itself from Google's own gstatic.com content-delivery network when opened, which is unrelated to this Service's own data and governed by Google's own policies, not this one.

9. Data Retention

We keep your account, map, and library data for as long as your account exists or the data serves the purposes above. You may request deletion of your account data at any time using the contact information below; we will delete what we control, other than records we're required to keep (e.g. for fraud prevention or legal compliance).

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Choices

You can review or delete the maps saved under your account from the "My Maps" section of index.html. To request a copy or deletion of any other data we hold about you, contact us below.

12. Security

We rely on Firebase Authentication and Firestore/Storage security rules to restrict who can write to your account's data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after a change is posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
