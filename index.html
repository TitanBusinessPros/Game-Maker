4d Missile Silo (optional)

A stationary long-range launcher. Works like Air Base above for the building itself - name it whatever fits (Missile Silo, Launch Bay…), give it its own art, HP, and build cost. The launcher you add below it is placed differently from Air Base's own units though: like a Gun Turret, building one arms a placement mode - click anywhere between 50 and 300 range of your Home Base to place it there. It never moves once placed, and never fires on its own: click it, then click an enemy, to send a missile at that target. The missile takes real time to travel there (set the missile's own speed per launcher, below) and locks onto whatever it was fired at - if that target is destroyed before the missile arrives, it just disappears from the screen instead of exploding on nothing. Its range starts at 500 and researches up to 1000 then 2000 in-game (see the Research tab) - not a field set here, same idea as Gold's own mining rate upgrading through research. Leave the launcher list empty and this whole feature just doesn't show up in-game.

4f Gun Turrets (optional)

Stationary defenses - not built next to your Home Base automatically like other units. Instead, in-game, building one puts you into a placement mode: click anywhere between 50 and 300 range of your Home Base to place it there. It never moves once placed (Speed is ignored for these), and fires on its own at whatever enemy comes into range. Add as many turret types as you want; leave the list empty and this feature just doesn't show up in-game.
